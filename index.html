About

The Duran Band is a tribute to the sound and style of Duran Duran—tight grooves, lush synths, soaring vocals, and infectious energy. Featuring Lisa A. Bivona and Eric Mooney on vocals, Armando Cuéllar on guitar, Ron West on keys, Jeff Wolinski on bass, and George Mexis on drums, the band delivers an authentic, high-energy live show that brings the iconic 80s hits back to the stage.
